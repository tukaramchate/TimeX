Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about TimeX watches and services

How long does shipping take?

+

Standard shipping typically takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping is available for 2-3 business days delivery. Orders are processed within 24 hours on business days.

Do you ship internationally?

+

Yes, we ship to most countries worldwide. International shipping times vary by destination, typically 10-15 business days. Additional customs fees may apply.

How can I track my order?

+

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can also track your order by clicking the "Track Order" button in the header or visiting your account dashboard.

Are your watches authentic?

+

Absolutely! All TimeX watches are 100% authentic and come with manufacturer warranties. We source directly from authorized distributors and manufacturers.

What is the warranty on watches?

+

All watches come with a 2-year manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. Luxury watches may have extended warranties. Physical damage and water damage are not covered.

Are the watches water-resistant?

+

Water resistance varies by model. Each product page lists the specific water resistance rating. Most analog watches are splash-resistant, while sport watches offer deeper water resistance ratings.

What is your return policy?

+

We offer a 7-day return policy from the date of delivery. Products must be unworn, in original packaging with all tags attached. Return shipping is free for defective items.

How do I initiate a return?

+

Contact our customer service team or visit your account dashboard to initiate a return. You'll receive a return label and instructions. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the item.

Can I exchange a product?

+

Yes, exchanges are available within 7 days. Contact our support team to arrange an exchange. The process is similar to returns, and we'll ship your new item once we receive the original.

What payment methods do you accept?

+

We accept all major credit/debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), UPI, net banking, and digital wallets. All transactions are secured with SSL encryption.

Is it safe to use my credit card?

+

Yes, absolutely. We use industry-standard SSL encryption to protect your payment information. We do not store your credit card details on our servers.

Do you offer EMI options?

+

Yes, EMI options are available on purchases above ₹5,000. You can choose 3, 6, 9, or 12-month EMI plans at checkout. EMI availability depends on your card issuer.

Still have questions?

Can't find the answer you're looking for? Our customer support team is here to help.
